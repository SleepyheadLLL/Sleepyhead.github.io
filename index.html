About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at Texas Tech University, advised by Dr. Tianxi Ji. I received my M.S. in Computer Science and Technology from Sichuan Normal University in 2021, advised by Prof. Chaosheng Feng, and my B.S. in Information and Computing Science from Sichuan Normal University.

Research focus. I develop privacy-preserving machine learning and data-analysis methods, with particular interests in differential privacy, federated learning, diffusion models, and cryptography.

Research Overview

“I develop principled methods that make learning and data sharing useful without compromising privacy.”

Private Learning and Data Analysis

Differential privacy and federated learning methods that preserve the utility of statistical and machine-learning systems.

Privacy-Preserving Generative Models

Privacy-aware diffusion and generative approaches, complemented by cryptographic techniques for secure data access and collaboration.
