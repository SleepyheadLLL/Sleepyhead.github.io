About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at Texas Tech University, advised by Dr. Tianxi Ji. I received my M.S. in Computer Science and Technology from Sichuan Normal University in 2021, advised by Prof. Chaosheng Feng, and my B.S. in Information and Computing Science from Sichuan Normal University. My research focuses on data security and privacy, tradeoffs among utility, privacy/security and efficiency, specifically differential privacy, federated learning, and the security of diffusion models.

Open to collaboration. I am currently seeking like-minded collaborators. If you are interested in my research, please feel free to contact me via email: Shuainan.Liu@ttu.edu.

Research Overview

My research lies at the intersection of privacy, security, and machine learning. I study differential privacy, federated learning, privacy-preserving generative models, and cryptographic techniques for secure data sharing. My goal is to develop principled methods that enable useful learning and data analysis without compromising individual privacy.

Private Learning and Data Analysis

Differential privacy and federated learning methods that preserve the utility of statistical and machine-learning systems.

Privacy-Preserving Generative Models

Privacy-aware diffusion and generative approaches, complemented by cryptographic techniques for secure data access and collaboration.
